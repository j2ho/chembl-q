# Tightly bound prosthetic groups: PDB chemical component ids.
#
# These stay bound to the protein and are not what a drug displaces. An
# inhibitor occupies an adjacent substrate site, so a pocket centred on one of
# these describes the cofactor site rather than the druggable site. They are
# therefore DEMOTED when one structure offers several ligands (see
# protein_filter.py), not excluded: a target whose every structure holds only a
# cofactor still gets its pocket from it, and the flag records that.
#
# NAD, NADP, SAM, SAH, ATP and the other co-substrates are deliberately ABSENT.
# They turn over, and whether an inhibitor competes with them or binds beside
# them is target-specific: in 1T2F NAD is the ligand that describes the site.
# Demoting them would move those pockets for no measured reason.
#
# Measured on the 1,506-target release: 87 targets had a pocket defined
# exclusively on a group listed here, and 60 of those had a drug-like ligand in
# the same structure that lost the ranking. Median distance from the chosen
# cofactor to that ligand was 8.5 A, against an 8 A pocket radius.
